What the words here mean

Running
The program is open on this Mac. On its own, it does nothing with your mail or Slack.
Watching
It reads new messages in the accounts your rules name, so the rules can act on them.
Paused
Watching stops for a while. Your rules, the Queue and anything waiting are kept.
Switched off
No rule acts until you switch watching on again, and approve it.
