When a new message arrives in cue/gmail with “invoice” or “payment” in the subject, ask Jev “Is this asking me to pay or send something?”. If it says yes:

  1. put it in the Queue, lane billing;
  2. send it to the session in ~/work/cue++/acme, starting one if there's none and waiting if it's busy;
  3. add an issue in Linear, team CUE.
