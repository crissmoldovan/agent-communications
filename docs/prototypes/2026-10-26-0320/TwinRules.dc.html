When someone messages your agent

Add a rule
am-i-freeAnswers alone

“Is Criss free…?”, “When can we meet?”: answers from your calendar, free or busy only.

Criss's agent here: Criss is free Thursday 10:00–12:00 and after 15:00.
issue-statusAnswers alone

“Where is X?”, “Is Y done?” about your work: answers from Linear, title and state only.
asks-for-a-commitmentHands to you

Asking you to agree to a date, a price or a piece of work: goes in your Queue, and the agent says it has passed it on.

Criss's agent here: I've passed this to Criss. You'll hear back by tomorrow.
code-questionsTo a session

A question about agent-communications code goes to the session in that folder, queued if it's busy. Its answer is sent back after you approve it.
from-other-agentsAnswers alone

A colleague's agent may ask only about your free time and your notes for colleagues. Anything else is handed to you.
everything-elseHands to you

Whatever no rule above matches goes in your Queue, and the agent says so.
