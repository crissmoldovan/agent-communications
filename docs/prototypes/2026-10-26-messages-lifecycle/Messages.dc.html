What the states mean

new
Waiting to be taken
No session has it yet. Any session that reads it may take it. If nobody does within 15 minutes, you’re reminded (Settings → Taking messages).
Taken by …
One session has it, and only that one may act on it: react, reply or mark it done. Others that read it see who has it, and leave it.
Handed back
A session took it, found it isn’t for it, and handed it back with its reason. It’s waiting to be taken again.
Answered
A reply went to its thread, after you approved it.
Done
The session that had it, or you, marked it done.

The summaries in “What it’s about” are written on this Mac (Settings → Search on this Mac). Open a message to see its own words.

Shown: messages in the accounts the event service watches, and any message a session read through Agent Comms. Kept 30 days. The message itself stays in Gmail or Slack. What you did in Gmail or Slack yourself isn’t here.
