Messages, from arrival to answer

Drawn for your feedback on 26 October, nothing built yet. Where a message came from, who took it, what each session did, and what one person asked; then projects, so a message reaches the right session. Open any card, or use the sidebar; every screen leads back here through Overview.

The life of a message, as drawn here

new
Arrives› A rule hands it to a session, or a session reads it itself› Waiting to be taken› Taken by one session› Answered› Done
orTaken› Handed back: “not mine”, with a reason› Waiting to be taken again

One new rule holds it together: a session must take a message before it reacts, replies or marks it done, so no two sessions answer the same message. Reading stays open to all of them; the others see who has it.
