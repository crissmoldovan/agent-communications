What sessions can do with a message

new
Take it
The answer is “taken” or “already taken by acme-site · Claude Code”. Only one session gets “taken”.
Hand it back
With a reason, such as “not mine: it’s about the website”. The reason shows in the message’s story and on Messages.
Ask who has it
Before it starts, so it doesn’t do work another session is doing.
Mark it done
With a line about what it did, which shows in the story.
Through Agent Comms Slack and Gmail, in every app you connected; the same at a terminal. A session that doesn’t take a message can still read it, and nothing else.
